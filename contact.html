FAQ

Common questions

A quick look at how Rafara works before you reach out.

How does Rafara Gate Pass work?+

Residents generate a digital pass for their expected visitor, who receives a QR code or OTP. Security staff scan or verify it at the gate for instant, logged entry.

Can Rafara be customized for my estate?+

Yes. Access rules, visitor categories, gate points, and notifications can all be configured to match how your estate already operates.

How do residents generate visitor passes?+

From the Rafara app, residents enter visitor details and a time window, then share the generated OTP directly with their guest.

Does Rafara work on mobile devices?+

Rafara is fully mobile-first, built for residents, security personnel, and estate managers on both Android and iOS.

How can my estate get started?+

Book a demo with our team, and we'll walk you through onboarding — including gate setup, staff training, and resident rollout.
