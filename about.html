Why we built Rafara

Estate security shouldn't depend on a phone call.

If you live in a gated estate in Nigeria, you probably know the routine.

A visitor arrives at the gate. The security guard calls your line. The network drops, your phone is unreachable, or you're in a meeting and can't pick up.

So the guard waits. The visitor waits. And sometimes, security is forced to make a decision without knowing for sure who they're letting in.

Rafara was created to make visitor access simpler, faster, and more secure for everyone inside a gated community.

We built Rafara because estate security shouldn't depend on a phone call.

What Rafara does

A code, not a call.

Rafara replaces the traditional phone call with a simple verification code. When you're expecting a visitor, you generate a secure 4-digit pass and share it with them. When they arrive at the gate, the security guard enters the code and instantly verifies the visit.

No missed calls. No unnecessary waiting. No guesswork.

Just a simple process that connects residents, visitors, and security. It's a small change to the way estates handle access, but one that can make everyday life much easier.

Built for estates

Built for estates, not just individuals.

Rafara isn't designed as another app that every household has to figure out on its own.

It's built for entire estates.

Estate management brings Rafara in as a shared security utility, giving residents and security personnel access to the same system from day one.

That means a more connected access process across the entire estate — without requiring individual residents to set up or pay for a separate security system.

Who's behind Rafara

Two girls who decided to build it themselves.

Rafara started with two girls who saw a problem around them and decided they didn't want to just complain about it — they wanted to build a solution.

Ire and Rahmah are the co-founders of Rafara. With a shared interest in technology, design, and building things that actually make a difference, they started Rafara with one simple question:

“Why can't estate security just work better?”

We didn't start with a huge team, a big office, or a complicated plan. We started by looking at a problem we understood, talking about how it could be better, and deciding to build it ourselves.

Rafara is our attempt to make estate access simpler for everyone — from the resident creating a pass, to the visitor arriving at the gate, to the security guard verifying it.

We're still building, learning, and figuring things out as we go. But one thing has always stayed the same:

We want to make things work.

And Rafara is just the beginning.
