Why we built Rafara

Estate security shouldn't depend on a phone call.

If you've lived in a gated estate in Nigeria, you probably know the routine.

A visitor arrives. Security calls the resident. The network drops, the phone is unreachable, or the resident simply can't pick up.

Rafara was created to remove that uncertainty and make visitor access faster, simpler, and more secure.

OUR PRINCIPLE

“Estate security should just work.”

What Rafara does

A code, not a call.

Residents create a secure 4-digit visitor pass. The visitor receives the code, and security verifies it at the gate.

Who's behind Rafara

Two girls who decided to build it themselves.

Rafara started with two girls who saw a problem around them and decided they didn't want to just complain about it — they wanted to build a solution.

Ire and Busola are the co-founders of Rafara. With a shared interest in technology, design, and building useful things, they started with one simple question:

“Why can't estate security just work better?”

We didn't start with a huge team or a complicated plan. We saw a problem, talked about how it could be better, and decided to build it ourselves.

We're still building and learning as we go. But one thing hasn't changed:

We want to make things work.
